if volume were a measure of emotion, every feeling i have would be a shout. i can't feel anything in whispers—hell, i don’t even want to. everything inside me has to come out in a roar, loud and unapologetic. there’s no soft hum in my heart, no quiet murmur of sentiment. everything inside me is a full-throttle shout, rattling the walls of my chest, the kind of thing that can’t be hidden, tucked away or ignored. emotions are explosions, fireworks—never gentle breezes or whispers. i don’t know how to feel anything without it taking over every inch of me.

it’s like a switch flips, and suddenly i’m drowning in it. i don’t get subtlety; i get full-on tidal waves that crash and crash until the shore is unrecognizable. even the small stuff feels big—like a drop of water landing on the skin and immediately becoming an ocean. i don’t know how to feel things lightly. everything i feel has to be an all-encompassing force, like a siren’s call, loud enough to tear through everything around me.

i guess that’s why i can’t stand the idea of quietly processing emotions. who needs that? who wants to sit with things softly, carefully? no, i want to rip through every feeling with the intensity of a thousand voices shouting at once. i want to feel my thoughts like they’re shaking the ground beneath me, like they’re too big to be contained. i’m not interested in the quiet hum of contemplation, the little beats of calmness. i want the roar, the wild intensity, the raw energy.

sometimes, i wonder if this is normal, but then i remember: who decides what's normal? maybe this is just me, living in a world where everything must be felt at full volume. emotions aren’t meant to be muted or dimmed down for anyone’s comfort. i’ve never been comfortable with that quiet, cautious approach to feeling. why would i even want to be? it feels too much like holding back, like living in a muffled version of myself. and that’s never been my style.

i guess i’m addicted to the chaos of it. there’s something liberating about not having to censor or soften the mess inside me. i can let it all spill out, raw and unfiltered, even if it makes the people around me uncomfortable. because who cares? i’m not here to be quiet for their sake. i’m here to be loud, to let every burst of emotion fly free, no matter the consequences. maybe that’s the price you pay for living out loud.

i’ve learned to live in the noise, to make peace with the way my feelings crash over me like waves. there’s no turning them down; they’re never on low, never on a setting that makes sense for anyone else. it’s just me and the volume, cranked up as high as it goes. when i feel something, it’s all-encompassing. and i’ve learned that it’s okay. i’ve learned that the world can handle it, even if it seems like too much at times.

maybe it’s because i’ve always been a little bit too much. too much energy, too much passion, too much noise. my emotions have always reflected that. maybe it’s because i want to be seen in full technicolor, not muted into some grayscale version of myself. i don’t want to be a whisper in a crowd, not when i can be the thunder. i want the impact, the force, the undeniable presence that comes with turning it all up to eleven.

and still, it feels like it’s never enough. no matter how loud i get, there’s always something more i want to scream into the world. more emotion, more feeling, more of everything. it’s like a hunger, this need to live louder, feel more. maybe that’s why i keep pushing the volume higher, chasing the next big thing, the next rush of feeling that makes me feel alive. i don’t know how to stop once it’s started. maybe i don’t even want to.
