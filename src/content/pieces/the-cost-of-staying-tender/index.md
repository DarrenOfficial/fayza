sometimes, the exhaustion runs deeper than just being tired. it feels like a quiet ache that lives in the bones. i recognize that there are things i still struggle with, aspects of myself that feel unfinished or flawed. and while i’m learning to accept that growth takes time, i also know that much of what i lack now was shaped by the pain i’ve carried from my past. it’s not an excuse, just a quiet truth i’ve had to live with. i am a work in progress and though i carry wounds, i also carry effort and sincerity.

what keeps me grounded through it all, is knowing that my heart has always been in the right place. i’ve never had bad intentions, never wanted to see the people around me suffer. if anything, i’ve always tried to love people gently, to care for them in ways i wish someone had cared for me. my love may not be perfect, but it has always been honest, quiet, patient, and hopefully tender. and that’s something i hold onto, even when things fall apart.

i give a lot. not just in words or gestures, but in presence, in listening, in believing that if i open up, people will stay. so when i choose to be vulnerable, when i finally let someone in and show them the messier parts of myself, it’s not done lightly. it’s terrifying but i do it because i believe in connection, in the power of being seen and accepted despite it all. i want to trust, even if it scares me every time.

more often than not, that trust gets met with silence. or distance. or worse, a slow fading of warmth. it hurts to pour so much of yourself into someone, only to feel like they emotionally check out once they’ve seen the parts of you that needed the most care. being emotionally neglected after finally opening up, after trusting isn’t just painful. it’s deeply disorienting. it makes me question whether the love i offer is too much, or maybe too little, or maybe just wrong altogether.

i’m left wondering if there’s something inherently off about how i love, how i care, how i exist in relationships. but then i come back to the one thing i do know: my love has always come from a pure place. i’ve never wanted to hurt anyone. all i’ve ever wanted was to build something meaningful, to offer the kind of care i never got when i needed it most. and maybe that’s the part that breaks me the most when that intention goes unseen or unreciprocated.

even with all the hurt, i still want to believe that it’s not wrong to care deeply. that it’s not weak to feel this much. i just wish people would see how hard it is to be soft in a world that often doesn’t know what to do with tenderness. i wish the love i gave didn’t feel so invisible once it’s handed over. because at the end of the day, i don’t need grand gestures just someone who holds space for me, the way i try to hold space for them.

this is the weight i carry, the ache of wanting to be gentle and loving, but also wanting to be safe. the confusion of feeling like my care is too much and not enough all at once. the quiet desperation to be held without being broken, to be loved without losing myself. and maybe the hardest part is living in a world that mistakes tenderness for weakness and turns compassion into a weapon against the one who dares to ask for it.
