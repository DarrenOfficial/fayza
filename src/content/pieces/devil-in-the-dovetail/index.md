the weight of it, the shape of it, too much and too little, a paradox pressing deep into the chest. a longing for it to end, but what does that even mean? over like the last note of a song, suspended in the air, fading into silence that never quite settles? over like the end of a season, when the cold lingers long after the last leaf has fallen, the earth still trembling beneath its own memory? it’s a strange thing, wanting something to end when it can’t even be named, when the beginning and the end blur into one endless moment, like trying to catch smoke with bare hands. the more it’s pulled, the more it tangles.

how can belief find its place when the line between self and not-self is so blurred? it’s like searching for the edge of a shadow, that place where light and dark should meet but never quite do, forever caught in a dance that never resolves. the doubt, the gnawing emptiness, feels like it’s always been there, buried beneath the skin, carved into the very bones. it’s touched and traced, but it’s not just surface. it’s in the marrow, the way the body bends, the way bodies splinter under its own weight, like a tree with branches reaching skyward, yet its roots are too entangled in the earth to rise. the weight of the past clings, a thousand years of soil and stone pressing down, keeping the roots anchored, keeping everything grounded in the dark.

there’s a devil in the dovetail, and maybe that’s where it all began. a small misalignment, a crack that whispered instead of screaming, a flaw so subtle it went unnoticed at first. it didn’t crash in, it slithered in, like a shadow in the corner of the eye, barely there until it’s too late. too busy trying to hold it all together, too busy convincing the mind that everything fits, that everything is fine. but now, all that’s left is the tension, the strain, the way the pieces don’t quite fit together, the way they never truly did. wondering if they ever could, or if the mind simply told itself they did because it was easier than facing the truth,that something has been held onto that was always slipping, always out of reach. the harder it’s tried, the more it slips away.

ratified by disgrace, the mark doesn’t fade, no matter how it’s concealed. it’s in the eyes, a truth that’s too raw to deny, too painful to fully face. the bloody frown, the hollow face—concealed in a disguise, a mask that shields the world from the weight beneath. it’s the kind of thing that never fully lets go, like a knot pulled too hard, pulling at the edges, on the verge of snapping apart. the months melt into the air, slipping through fingers like sand, but the image remains, clear and sharp, like a scar that won’t heal. and still, that grace lingers, too beautiful to ignore, too painful to embrace. it’s been there, all along, anchored in the spaces between now and then, every breath. and through it all, there’s been a sense of being lost, of trying to find a way back to something, anything, that might make sense of it all. but the path forward is clouded, the journey uncertain, and still, the grace lingers, a reminder of what could have been, what still might be, even when everything feels like it’s already fallen apart.

it’s hard to believe in something that feels like it’s always on the edge of falling apart. maybe that’s the curse of it, forever teetering, never steady, like holding onto something that was never meant to stay. trying to find some sense in the chaos, but the cracks aren’t signs of holding, they’re the marks of something that was never whole to begin with, no matter how tightly it’s been kept together. the struggle isn’t a battle to win, but the quiet weight of knowing it’s already unraveling, the effort to move forward when the path ahead is nothing but a blur, swallowed by shadows of everything that’s come before. maybe belief isn’t even the point. maybe it’s the fear of letting go, the dread of what happens when it’s done, when the pieces finally give in to the pressure and scatter, when everything held so tightly slips away and leaves nothing but the hollow echo of what once was.

the lines of the dovetail traced, where the pieces meet but never merge, where the fractures widen, where the devil’s presence lingers, faint but always there. it’s not allowed to settle, the imperfection gnawing, a constant reminder that nothing can ever be whole, nothing can ever belong. the beauty isn’t here, not in this rupture, but in the slow, suffocating pull of everything slipping away, where the gaps between the pieces are nothing but hollow space, where things crumble and never reassemble. it’s the story of a being breaking, of friction wearing down what little is left, of growth that feels more like loss, more like the fading of something that never had a chance to be. and maybe that’s all there is now. to sit with the weight of it, to feel it press, to not know what’s left, to not know where to turn or how to go on, like a river whose current has lost its way, dragging everything with it, unsure of where it’s headed, if it’s headed anywhere at all.
