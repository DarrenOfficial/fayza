today unfolded in a way that didn’t ask for attention but demanded presence. i worked only until late morning, gone to my therapy session, went home, and slept longer than i expected to. when i woke up, my body was already carrying something heavy, the kind of weight that doesn’t announce itself loudly but spreads slowly through the chest, the shoulders, and the stomach. i cried before i could make sense of it, not because something specific happened, but because my body was already responding to a buildup i hadn’t consciously named yet. ah… right, i hadn’t taken my medicine. i let myself sit with the feeling instead of rushing to fix it.

going out to jog felt less like a choice and more like a response. staying still inside my body felt unbearable in a quiet, persistent way, as if everything inside me needed somewhere to move. the beginning of the run was uncomfortable. my legs felt stiff, my breathing uneven, and my thoughts kept drifting back to the discomfort, trying to negotiate with it or find a reason to stop. i noticed how quickly my system wanted relief rather than tolerance, how instinctively i searched for an exit from sensation instead of staying with it.

so i shifted my attention, not by forcing calm, but by grounding myself in what was physically real. i paid attention to the ground beneath my shoes and the repeated transfer of weight from one foot to the other. i noticed the air moving against my skin and entering my lungs, sometimes shallow, sometimes deeper, without me having to control it. every few minutes, i reminded myself where i was, that i was safe, that nothing was required of me except to keep going at a pace my body could handle.

as i kept running, waves of emotion surfaced without turning into full thoughts. there was a tightness in my throat, a heaviness in my chest, a hollow feeling that came and went. instead of labeling it or pushing it away, i stayed present with the sensation and let my body move through it. at some point, something my therapist said earlier that day came back to me. she explained that the work i’m doing right now isn’t about becoming stronger or more resilient in a performative way, but about increasing my capacity. learning how to feel more without becoming overwhelmed, and discovering that my system is capable of holding discomfort without collapsing or shutting down.

running made that idea tangible. with every step, my body wasn’t trying to escape the pain but to process it. movement gave it a way to metabolize what had been stored, allowing the sensation to spread out instead of staying compressed in one place. gradually, my breathing began to deepen on its own, my shoulders loosened, and my legs stopped feeling like they were carrying an extra weight that didn’t belong to them.

time became less important as i continued. i wasn’t tracking minutes or distance, and i wasn’t chasing clarity or relief. i was simply staying with myself as the experience unfolded. when i eventually slowed down and made my way home, the quiet felt different, not because the pain was gone, but because it no longer felt threatening. i was able to eat, sit, and let my body settle without the urgency to distract myself from what i was feeling.

the ache returned in a softer form, and i cried again, this time without panic or the sense that something needed to be resolved immediately. i thought about how my therapist described growth, not as the absence of pain, but as the ability to remain present while the feeling moves through the body. tonight, that understanding felt embodied rather than intellectual. i could feel the difference in how i was holding myself, how i was breathing, how i was staying instead of disappearing.

i’m beginning to understand that my body isn’t something that needs to be controlled or corrected. it’s learning alongside me, responding to consistency, patience, and care. moments like this don’t feel triumphant or clean, but they offer proof that something real is shifting. i didn’t abandon myself when things became uncomfortable. i let the experience complete itself in its own time.

today didn’t give me answers or closure, but it gave me evidence. evidence that i can stay with my body through discomfort, that i can move through pain without losing myself inside it, and that this slow, uneven process is still progress. for now, that feels like enough to trust.
