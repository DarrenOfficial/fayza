# shattered poise,

The personal website of Fayza Divanti at sorrowflux.com, an archive of her writing presented as a scrapbook.

## Language

### Identity

**shattered poise,**:
The publication name (lowercase, with the trailing comma). It is the masthead of the site.
_Avoid_: shattered poise (without the comma), Shattered Poise

**Fayza Divanti**:
The author. She is shown under the masthead as "by Fayza Divanti".
_Avoid_: fay, sorrowflux (as a person's name)

**sorrowflux**:
The domain and Substack handle. It is never used as a display name.

### Writing

**Piece**:
One work of hers on the site, such as an essay or a prose poem. It was originally published on Substack.
_Avoid_: post, article, entry, blog

**Shelf**:
One of three curated groupings, and every Piece sits on exactly one. The three Shelves are *tender things* (grief, healing, love, self-worth), *against the grain* (political and feminist essays) and *songs & tributes* (Pieces built around a song or an artist).
_Avoid_: category, tag, theme

**Language stamp**:
The EN or ID mark that shows which language a Piece is written in. English and Indonesian Pieces share one chronological feed.
_Avoid_: locale, translation

**Cover**:
The image that opens a Piece. Every Piece has one.
_Avoid_: thumbnail, hero image

### Places

**Wall**:
The homepage. It is a scrapbook collage of every Piece and can be filtered by Shelf.
_Avoid_: feed, blog, archive, index

**Fig. label**:
The small numbered mono caption (for example "FIG. 07" or "NO. 012") pinned beside an image or card on the Wall.
_Avoid_: badge, tag
